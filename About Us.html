ABOUT US

At ABI&Co., we believe that every great event starts with a story. Whether it's a milestone celebration or a high-level corporate gathering, our goal is to translate your vision into reality. We combine carefull planning with grand design to ensure that your event isn't just attended, it's remembered.

Crafting Moments that Define Excellence.

Who We Are

ABI&Co is an event organization and design firm based in Addis Ababa. Founded on the principles of creativity, precision, and purpose, we specialize in coordinating large-scale community events, professional networking experiences, and private celebrations. Our team of experts handles every detail from complex logistics and vendor management to aesthetic design allowing you to focus on what truly matters, your guests.

Vision

To set the standard in event design and organization, where every detail is a masterpiece and every event serves as a benchmark for excellence in the industry.

Mission

To deliver seamless, high-quality event solutions by blending strategic planning with creative design, ensuring every client's objective is met with excellence and elegance.
